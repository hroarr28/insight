# Field receipts

Field staff turn receipts in to the yard desk within five working days of the spend. The desk stamps the date received. A receipt older than five working days is still filed, and the weekly sheet notes that it arrived late.

A meal receipt over 18 dollars needs the host's initials on the paper before the desk will take it. The initials match the host named on that day's yard log. Tips are part of the 18 dollar figure. A meal at or under 18 dollars needs no initials.

A fuel receipt must show the vehicle number in the corner, written by the driver before the paper reaches the desk. The number matches the plate list on the yard board. If the number is missing, the desk puts the receipt in the hold folder and does not enter it on the weekly sheet. Staff do not replace a missing receipt with a guessed amount. The weekly sheet gets a short note that the paper was not turned in.
