# Visitor badges

Helmfell Yard keeps a desk at the front gate. A visitor signs the paper log and receives a yellow badge on a clip. The badge shows the visit date and the host's name. It is worn on the outer layer, where a person at the gate can read it without asking the visitor to open a coat.

The visitor returns the yellow badge to the yard desk before leaving the yard. A badge left in a cab or carried past the gate is reported the same day. The desk writes the badge number in the lost column of the log. A replacement is issued only after that line is filled in.

A visitor does not walk the yard alone. The host named on the badge stays with the visitor from the gate until the badge is back on the desk. The cold room stays closed to visitors unless that host goes through the door with them. A yellow badge on its own does not open the cold room.
