# Cold room entry

The cold room on the north wall is held between 2°C and 4°C. The dial beside the door is read at the start of each shift. If the dial shows 5°C or higher, stored goods stay out of the room until the dial is back inside that range. The person who reads the dial writes the number on the shift sheet.

The door stays shut except while someone is stepping through. It is not held open with a stop. Anyone who stays inside for more than fifteen minutes writes the entry time and the exit time on the wall sheet before leaving.

A visitor comes in only with the host named on the yellow badge. The host goes in first and comes out last. A probe check inside the room is taken at the centre shelf, away from the door fan. A reading taken beside the door is marked void on the shift sheet and taken again at the centre shelf.
